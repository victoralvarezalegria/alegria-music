7. THE CALENDLY TO BOOKED-PAGE MECHANISM. Keep it exactly.
==================================================================

apply.html embeds Calendly inline. When a visitor books, Calendly posts a `calendly.event_scheduled` message to the page. The page's script:
  1. checks the message origin is https://calendly.com,
  2. posts the event and invitee URIs to /api/book,
  3. stores the returned first name in sessionStorage as `va_name`,
  4. redirects to /booked.
This works on Calendly's free plan and needs no paid redirect setting. Keep the whole `window.addEventListener('message', ...)` block and the `calendly.page_height` handling (it resizes the calendar so it never scrolls inside its own box). Do not replace it with Calendly's "redirect to external site" option.

booked.html greets the visitor from `va_name`. If it ever arrives with `invitee_full_name` / `invitee_email` / `event_start_time` in the URL, it posts those to /api/book itself and scrubs them from the address bar. Keep that too.
